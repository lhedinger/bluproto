compare genomes

Two genomes side by side, read like a code diff. One number on top: the mean of a body similarity (one minus each gene's distance across its own range, averaged) and a mind similarity (the share of the longer program that the two share, instruction for instruction). Both halves are shown beside it, so the mean never has to be trusted alone. A clade difference is reported as its own fact and does not move the number. Load each side from a creature's id, a .genome file or a pasted line; from the world, the inspector's compare button fills A with one creature and B with the next.
