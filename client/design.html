The viewer's chrome built from the facility's own materials: bulkhead steel for surfaces, signal red for the action that commits, indicator lamps for state, hazard yellow for anything that destroys. Controls are furniture. Buttons are plates that sink when pressed, toggles are lamps, sliders ride a rail. Every edge is a 2 px bevel on the art-pixel lattice with the corners left open, and nothing is rounded or blurred.

Bevel grammar

raised · lit top and left, shadow bottom and right. Panels, buttons, toasts.
sunk · the reverse. Wells: fields, tracks, switches, readouts.
pressed · a raised plate drops 2 px and its bevel flips. Active and toggled-on.
hazard · stripes only on things that destroy. Reset, delete, cull.

Layout & breakpoints the viewer at three widths

Breakpoints are widths of the viewer root, so everything in the viewer switches together, and the mock below switches when you change its width. Panels keep the viewer's four anchors from the panels skill: card, menu, half and full. On a phone the corner panels become sheets from the bottom edge, controls take the 30 px touch height (24 px small), and dialogues dock to the bottom.
