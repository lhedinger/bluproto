the body

The body as physics: mass and everything that scales from it, the price of existing, moving, growing and breeding, the needs that drive it all, and — closing the page — the body's veto: the hard constraints a mind's actuators can ask past but never push past. Each figure is read off the running simulation's own constants, never transcribed. What a lineage inherits — genes, clades, minds, senses and acts — lives on the genome page.
