help

How the world looks and, in time, how it works. Everything drawn here is drawn by the web client's own code, driven by scripted state — the same drawing this browser puts on the live viewer, reading the same server-baked atlases — so what is on this page is what a viewer actually sees rather than a second rendering that resembles it. A picture baked down some other path would only be a picture of what the art ought to be. Ground itself is catalogued separately: /tiles lists every tile type — names, flags, variants, and the rules of how tiles fit together — baked by the server, because for ground the bake is the live rendering. The rules run deeper on two pages of their own: /help/genome covers everything a lineage inherits — genes, clades, minds, senses and acts — and /help/body the body's books and the hard limits no mind can override.
